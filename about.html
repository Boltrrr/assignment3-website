About ISO-CORE

ISO-CORE is an indie open-world isometric game currently in development. The project focuses on exploration, crafting, survival, and player creativity in a procedurally generated world. Players will be able to gather resources, construct structures, and defend themselves against hostile creatures while building their own unique experience.

The goal of ISO-CORE is to combine the relaxing freedom of sandbox building games with the challenge of exploration and survival. Each world will offer new opportunities for discovery, encouraging players to experiment with different strategies and playstyles.

The Developer

ISO-CORE is being developed by JohnBrx, an independent creator with a passion for game development and creative programming. The project began as a personal passion project exploring procedural worlds, player-driven gameplay, and sandbox-style design.

Development updates, experiments, and progress videos are occasionally shared on the JohnBrx YouTube channel, where followers can see how the game evolves over time.
